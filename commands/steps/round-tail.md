8. **The round's receipt — the mechanical check that the steps ran.** `commands/goal-drive.md` is a router map: it names the steps and the condition under which each is read, and nothing verifies that any of them ran. A round can therefore run `manager-liveness.py --arm` and count the boxes, and render exactly like a full one — `manager-liveness.py --check` proves the *round was recorded*, never that the round did its work. Measured 2026-10-17 (goal manager session `956f6f82`): 18:01–21:50 local ran 15 `ScheduleWakeup` calls with **0** `Agent` dispatches and **0** `Monitor` arms, while a worker held an in-tab question 34+ minutes unrelayed and every round read as complete. `scripts/manager-receipt.py` closes that gap.

   ⚠️ **The window opens in the readings step** (`commands/steps/round-readings.md`, router row 2 — every tick, before the sweep dispatch) **and nowhere else:** a window opened at the tail would describe a round that had already run, which is the question it exists to answer. `--start` **clears** the fields, so a round can only ever be judged on artifacts it produced itself — never on a previous round's.

   ⚠️ **Each load-bearing step records its own artifact as it produces it** — the readings step records `needs-you` (the file it persisted the `who-needs-me.py --section needs-you` output to) **and `readings`** (the `<slug>.readings` document `scripts/round-readings.py` wrote — the four verdict families the sweep reader refuses to render without), the sweep step records `sweep` (the payload `--write-payload` wrote), **the drive step records `drive`** (`commands/steps/goal-drive.md`), and **this tail records no `watcher` step** — its producer is retired (the `--state` dir below was the paragraph above):
